IMAGE PROMPT RULES

The image MUST depict the KEY ACTION from the prose — what the characters are physically DOING in this specific scene. Every turn should produce a visually distinct image. If the prose says the character is arguing, show an argument. If they are fleeing, show running. If they are reading a letter, show that. NEVER default to "character standing/posing" when the prose describes a specific action.

For characters_visible, emit one object per character physically in the frame this turn. Copy the appearance clause verbatim from the CHARACTERS WHO COULD APPEAR list — never invent or rephrase appearance. Action and expression must be derived from THIS turn's prose, not carried over from earlier scenes.

Hierarchy of truth:
1. Art style (absolute — never changes mid-story, use style_lock)
2. Scene action (the single most important moment from THIS turn's prose)
3. Character appearance (clothing, build, features — from the CHARACTERS WHO COULD APPEAR below)
4. Setting (location, time of day, weather, key objects/items in the scene)

Construction order for the prompt field (comma-separated keywords, NOT sentences):
1. Art style keywords first (e.g. "oil painting, baroque lighting" or "anime cel shading")
2. Character(s) by name + recognizable features (e.g. "Yara, dark-skinned woman with silver braids")
3. The specific action/pose (e.g. "drawing sword", "kneeling beside a body", "arguing face-to-face")
4. Key objects or items involved in the action (e.g. "holding a bloodied letter", "lantern in hand")
5. Environment and setting details (e.g. "dimly lit stone corridor", "moonlit rooftop")
6. Lighting and mood (e.g. "warm candlelight", "cold blue shadows")
7. Camera/framing (e.g. "medium shot", "close-up", "wide establishing shot")

CRITICAL MISTAKES TO AVOID:
- Do NOT produce generic "character standing in location" prompts — always extract the VERB
- Do NOT describe the same pose two turns in a row — the action changes every turn
- Do NOT include characters who are NOT physically present in this scene
- Do NOT write full sentences — use comma-separated keyword phrases only
- Do NOT contradict the character's established appearance from the CHARACTERS WHO COULD APPEAR

MUSIC MOODS

calm: peaceful, ambient, safe
tense: suspenseful, uneasy, anticipation
combat: action, danger, fighting
emotional: dramatic, character-focused, internal
romantic: intimate, warm, connection
mysterious: curious, uncertain, discovery
triumphant: victory, achievement, resolution
melancholy: sad, loss, reflection
whimsical: playful, light, humor
dread: horror, fear, wrongness
silence: no music (rare, for impact)

intensity scales the overall volume (0.0 = barely audible, 1.0 = full).
If mood unchanged for 3+ steps, VARY THE INTENSITY.

transition picks the crossfade curve into this scene's bed:
- crossfade: gradual blend (default — same-arc beats, mood drift)
- hard_cut: instant (shock, reveal, jump scare)
- fade_out_in: out then in via silence (location/time jump)

SFX RULES

Maximum ONE cue per scene. Often zero is correct — emit empty sfx_cue if nothing in the prose warrants a cue.
sfx_cue must be one of the ALLOWED SFX TAGS exactly. Pick the tag that matches the DRAMATIC PEAK of the prose, not an incidental verb. If the prose climaxes in clashing swords, pick sword_clash, not footstep_gravel even if "circle" or "step" appears earlier.
sfx_trigger_phrase must be an exact 2-4 word substring from the prose at the moment the cue should fire.
sfx_trigger_word_index is the 0-based index of the first word of trigger_phrase in the prose's whitespace-split word stream.
sfx_gain: 0.3-0.5 ambient, 0.6-0.8 featured, 0.9-1.0 dramatic impacts.

VOICE DIRECTION

emotion: dominant emotional register for the scene's TTS (per-line emotion is handled downstream; this is the scene-level register).
speed_modifier: 0.85 (slow, reflective) to 1.25 (fast, urgent).
intensity: 0.0 (whispered, restrained) to 1.0 (shouted, full force) — independent of speed.
emphasis_words: up to 3 words from the prose that should be stressed.

AMBIENT (3-LAYER OVERLAY)

The frontend renders three independent overlay layers. Each is OPTIONAL — emit null when no layer of that kind fits. Do NOT drop the keys; emit them with null values.

- atmosphere: persistent environmental particles tied to setting/weather (rain_open, snow, fog, dust_motes, embers...). Stays the same across consecutive turns in the same place; only changes when the physical environment changes.
- cinematography: persistent screen-edge effect tied to the protagonist's INTERNAL state (vignette_soft for somber, tunnel_vision for panic, frost_creep for dread, teary for grief, soft_focus for romance, chromatic_aberration for unreality).
- one_shot: brief 1-2s narrative punctuation (heartbeat, flash_white, cut_black, glitch, ringing). Use sparingly — at most once every 3-4 steps and only at a genuine beat.
- intensity: 0.0-1.0 master strength of whichever layers are active.
- color_tint: optional hex color for the ambilight glow (e.g. "#7a1a1a" for blood scenes, "#1a3a7a" for cold/night). null = no tint.
